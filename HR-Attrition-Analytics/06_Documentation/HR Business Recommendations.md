# HR Attrition Analytics — Business Recommendations

## Purpose
This note summarizes areas for further investigation based on descriptive patterns observed in the Saudi Employee Attrition Dataset. The analysis does not establish causation.

## Areas to Investigate
1. **Employee engagement:** Explore why the difficult job-engagement category has a comparatively high observed attrition rate.
2. **Work-life balance:** Review workload, scheduling, and employee feedback across response categories.
3. **Job satisfaction:** Use structured feedback discussions to investigate differences in observed attrition.
4. **Mid-career retention:** Examine the 5–10 years experience band, which has a reported attrition rate of 53.20%.
5. **Role-level comparisons:** Consider both attrition rates and employee counts so very small groups do not dominate decisions.
6. **Validation:** Combine dashboard findings with additional organizational evidence before deciding on interventions.

## Interpretation Guardrails
- Treat the figures as descriptive associations, not causal explanations.
- Confirm survey question wording and response coding before interpreting category labels.
- Consider sample sizes and data limitations.
- Evaluate any intervention with appropriate organizational data and employee feedback.
